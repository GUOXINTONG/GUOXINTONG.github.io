ICLR 2027 submissionMODEL & SYSTEM

AURA: Always-On Understanding and Real-Time Assistance via Video Streams

Xudong Lu*, Yang Bo*, Jinpeng Chen*, Shuhan Li*, Xintong Guo*, Huankang Guan*, Fang Liu, Dunyuan Xu, Peiwen Sun, Heyang Sun, Rui Liu, Hongsheng Li

* Equal contribution · Co-first authors

An end-to-end streaming visual interaction framework for continuous observation, real-time question answering, and proactive responses.

Paper ↗Code ↗Streaming VideoLLM / Proactive interaction
Research summary

AURA combines streaming context management, a coarse-to-fine data engine, a silent-speech balanced training objective, and an inference framework for long-horizon interaction.

ICML 2026BENCHMARK

PhoStream: Benchmarking Real-World Streaming for Omnimodal Assistants in Mobile Scenarios

Xudong Lu, Huankang Guan, Yang Bo, Jinpeng Chen, Xintong Guo, Shuhan Li, Fang Liu, Peiwen Sun, Xueying Li, Wei Zhang, Xue Yang, Rui Liu, Hongsheng Li

A mobile-centric benchmark for audio-visual understanding and response timing across on-screen and off-screen scenarios.

Paper ↗Code ↗Omnimodal evaluation / Response timing
Research summary

PhoStream evaluates open-ended responses with an online inference pipeline. It contains 5,572 QA pairs from 578 videos and probes instant, backward, and forward temporal reasoning.

ECCV 2026BENCHMARK

X-Stream: Exploring MLLMs as Multiplexers for Multi-Stream Understanding

Peiwen Sun, Xudong Lu, Huadai Liu, Yang Bo, Dongming Wu, Huankang Guan, Minghong Cai, Jinpeng Chen, Xintong Guo, Shuhan Li, Fang Liu, Rui Liu, Xiangyu Yue

A benchmark for continuous reasoning across concurrent video streams, studying MLLMs through the lens of signal multiplexing.

Paper ↗Project ↗Multi-stream reasoning / Multiplexing
Research summary

X-Stream comprises 4,220 QA pairs across 932 videos and 11 subtasks. Its multi-window, multi-view, and multi-device settings require evidence from multiple streams.

ICLR 2027 submissionBENCHMARK

FastBench: Can Streaming VLMs Perceive High-Dynamic Real-World Streams?

Yuxuan Hu, Weikang Shi, Yang Bo, Xudong Lu, Xintong Guo, Shuhan Li, Yuyang He, Huankang Guan, Peiwen Sun, Yunqiao Yang, Wenbo Li, Rui Liu, Hongsheng Li

Evaluating high-dynamic perception in real-world streams and adaptive temporal sampling under bounded context budgets.

Paper ↗Code ↗High-dynamic perception / Adaptive sampling
Research summary

FastBench contains 306 QA pairs across eight domains and six capabilities, with annotated evidence intervals. Its ProactiveFrame baseline adapts incoming frame rates and uses a dual-tier sliding window.
